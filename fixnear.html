FixNear

Discover, compare, book, and track local service professionals.

01 / Overview

Product Overview

FixNear is a comprehensive desktop web concept designed to resolve the daily frustration of finding reliable local home service professionals — including electricians, plumbers, appliance technicians, and home maintenance experts. By prioritizing upfront price transparency, distance awareness, verified ratings, and prototype live tracking, FixNear brings structure to an otherwise unstructured informal market.

02 / Problem

The Core Problem

Booking home repairs is currently filled with friction, lack of clarity, and anxiety:

  • Pricing Uncertainty: Homeowners often do not know whether a repair will cost 200 or 2,000 until the technician is already inside the house.
  • Unclear Availability: Endless phone calls back and forth simply to ask "Can you come by this evening?"
  • Zero Status Visibility: Once a booking is agreed over the phone, there is no confirmation, no reminder, and no way to know if the professional is actually on their way.
  • Fragmented Comparisons: No centralized place to evaluate verified ratings, experience, and proximity across nearby professionals.
03 / Goal

Design Goals

The mission for FixNear was to design an intuitive desktop experience that makes comparing and booking local service providers as seamless and predictable as modern e-commerce.

Transparent Trust Signals

Display upfront hourly or diagnostic rates, verified customer ratings, and distance right on the provider card before requiring a click.

Frictionless Scheduling

Streamline the booking workflow into a concise date/time slot picker paired with a real-time booking summary card.

Post-Booking Reassurance

Design a clear live tracking status screen that keeps customers informed of their technician's arrival window.

Predictable Navigation

Maintain consistent desktop web headers, filters, and standard breadcrumbs so users never feel lost.

04 / Target Users

Target Users

FixNear addresses everyday homeowners and tenants navigating urgent or routine maintenance:

  • Busy Working Professionals: People who need an electrician or plumber at a precise evening or weekend time slot rather than waiting all day.
  • Tenants & First-Time Homeowners: Individuals who do not have established personal networks of local handymen and need verified credentials.
  • Property Managers & Families: Users managing multiple household repairs who require transparent receipts and predictable scheduling.
05 · 06 / Qualitative Research

Research Insights & Information Priorities

Through qualitative analysis of how people search for local services, I found that trust is the decisive factor. When booking a stranger to enter a home, users look for specific reassurance cues in a particular psychological sequence:

The Trust Sequence Heuristic

Users evaluate: Who is the provider? → Are they rated well? → What is their specialty? → How far away are they? → When are they free? → What is the price?

Information Priority Rule

Ordering information to match this mental model: Provider → Rating → Service → Distance → Availability → Pricing → Booking CTA prevents cognitive overload.

09 / Wireframes

Low-Fidelity Layouts & Structure

Desktop wireframing focused on taking advantage of wide displays without spreading content so thin that users lose orientation:

  • Two-Column Booking Layout: On the booking page, the form controls sit on the left (60%) while a sticky Booking Summary card stays anchored on the right (40%), providing constant price transparency.
  • Grid-Based Listing: Cards feature consistent height, aligned star ratings, and clear hierarchy so comparing two plumbers takes seconds.

12 / Accessibility

Accessibility & Ergonomics

Color Contrast & Text Legibility

All body copy conforms to WCAG AA/AAA standards with #0F172A on #FFFFFF. Critical status states (such as active booking) use dual signaling (icon badge + text label).

Keyboard Operability

All provider listing cards, filters, and booking buttons have explicit :focus-visible rings and logical Tab index ordering.

13 / Prototype

Figma Prototype Interactions

The prototype was connected in Figma to demonstrate the booking journey:

  • Card Selection: On Click → Navigate To Provider Profile with Smart Animate (300ms ease-out).
  • Booking Flow: Step changes with smooth lateral position easing.
  • Interactive Tracking: Timeline node animation demonstrating provider progress from "Accepted" to "On the Way".
14 / Usability Testing

Qualitative Testing Walkthrough

I tested the prototype with peer users tasked with booking a drain repair technician in under 3 minutes:

Successes Observed

Users praised having the price estimate displayed directly on the listing card rather than having to begin checkout to uncover the diagnostic fee.

Iterative Refinement

During early tests, users missed the cancellation policy link. I moved the cancellation policy disclaimer into the primary Booking Summary box for complete clarity.

15 · 16 / Challenges & Solutions

Key Challenges & Solutions

Challenge: Dense Information Layouts

Service cards required showing provider name, photo, star rating, verified badge, hourly rate, estimated distance, and next available slot without looking crowded.

Solution: Established a strict typographic hierarchy (Inter SemiBold 20px for name, 13.5px mono for metadata) and subtle card separators.

Challenge: Anxiety Around Booking Arrival

Users expressed apprehension about whether a booked professional would actually show up.

Solution: Designed a dedicated "Track Your Service" screen featuring an estimated arrival countdown and direct call/message controls.

17 / Limitations

Limitations — Stated Honestly

Concept Prototype Notice

FixNear is a design concept and interactive Figma prototype, not a production application. There is no real backend, provider database, live GPS tracking, payment gateway, authentication server, or booking database. All data shown is placeholder — including provider names, ratings, pricing, and the customer quotes visible on the home screen screenshot. The map and tracking views are prototype visual representations.

18 · 19 · 20 / Retrospective

Outcome, Learnings & Next Steps

Qualitative Outcome

FixNear demonstrates how product design thinking can bring dignity, clarity, and trust to local trades. By structuring provider comparisons and tracking visually, the platform transforms a stressful task into a straightforward transaction.

Key Learnings

Designing for desktop web taught me how to handle generous viewports effectively using CSS Grid and sticky sidebar patterns. It also reinforced that visual hierarchy is the single most important factor when presenting high-density comparison data.

Future Planned Work (Future Integrations, Not Built)

  • Real-time provider availability engine connected via WebSockets.
  • Integration with live GPS mapping APIs (Google Maps / Mapbox) for realistic turn-by-turn technician tracking.
  • Secure payment gateway integration (Razorpay / Stripe) and verified customer review authentication.
  • Two-way provider management dashboard for service professionals to manage their jobs and earnings.
